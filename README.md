# NANDO Medical Devices — Premium affiliate-ready website

This version adds a curated product catalogue for international/China-made medical-device sourcing and affiliate-link placeholders.

## Important
The six `AFFILIATE_LINK_*` values in `index.html` are placeholders. Replace them with the actual tracking/deep links generated inside your approved affiliate account. Do not paste ordinary supplier URLs if you expect commission tracking.

Alibaba.com currently operates an official affiliate program. Its affiliate documentation says affiliates can generate deep links to product, category, supplier and promotion URLs, and that valid purchases can be tracked for up to 60 days. Commission rules can change, so use the terms shown in your affiliate account.

## Product/regulatory approach
This site is intentionally written as a product-discovery/sourcing platform, not as a statement that every listed device is CE-certified or approved in the EU.

If NANDO directly imports or distributes devices in the EU, obtain specialist regulatory advice. EU MDR assigns specific responsibilities to manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers and distributors. An EU-based operator obtaining a device directly from a non-EU manufacturer and placing it on the Union market can become the importer.
