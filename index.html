QUALITY & REGULATORY

We separate product discovery from regulatory approval.

A product appearing on NANDO does not by itself mean that NANDO has certified, approved, clinically evaluated or endorsed the product.

For EU-facing activity, economic operators can have defined responsibilities under the Medical Device Regulation (MDR). Products imported directly from a non-EU manufacturer can create importer obligations.
